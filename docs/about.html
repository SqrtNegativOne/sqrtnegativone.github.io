It is my lifelong goal to continue chasing that high of looking at a finished product, understanding fully the intricacies of the system (for you only created it) and feeling your brain be molded by it. So I continue to challenge myself in new ways, with new technologies, and new tools.

I am fluent in intermediate Python, can work in advanced Python, and am familiar with the basics of data structures and algorithms in C, C++, and Python. I am currently learning machine learning, and practicing to perfect my knowledge of data structures and algorithms with C++.

Other than programming itself, my hobbies include reading books, reading long-form essays on the web, and playing puzzle and story based games.
